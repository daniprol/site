Mathematics taught me to read with a pencil. The margin is where a proof becomes yours.<Sidenote>Fermat's most famous note was, fittingly, a margin note that didn't fit.</Sidenote> When I moved most of my reading into Neovim, I lost that margin, so I built one back.

sidenote.nvim shows notes as LSP diagnostics beside the line they belong to. They stay out of the file, travel with the project, and resurface on a schedule so they actually stick.
