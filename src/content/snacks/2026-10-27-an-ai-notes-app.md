Sample snack. Lovely onboarding: soft gradients, a friendly mascot and a promise to remember everything.

Then I asked it to summarise one meeting note, and it asked for my calendar, contacts, email and location first, with no way to grant less.

Uninstalled before the summary loaded. A notes app should start with my notes.
