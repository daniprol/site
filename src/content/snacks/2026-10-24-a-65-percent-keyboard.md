Sample snack. A week on a small mechanical keyboard with no function row and no numpad. The typing itself is lovely: short travel to the mouse, and my shoulders noticed by day two.

The cost shows up in the debugger. Every step-over is now a two-key chord, and I still reach for a key that isn't there.

Maybe. If I can remap the chords into something my hands accept without thinking, it stays on the desk.
