Sample snack. pnpm 11 stops packages from running their build scripts unless you have approved them. It is the right default: most of the supply-chain scares of the last two years started in a postinstall script.

The cost shows up the first time you install an old project. I had eleven packages asking for approval, and I could not tell you what half of them do at install time. I approved the ones I recognised and left the rest, and everything still worked, which says something.

I am keeping it on a branch for now. If the allowlist stays short on real projects it gets merged. If I catch myself approving things without reading, it is theatre and I will go back.
