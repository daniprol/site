Sample snack. The new build-script allowlist is the right default. Packages no longer run install scripts unless you say so, which closes a real supply chain hole.

The first install of an older project is a wall of approvals, though: fourteen prompts for esbuild, sharp and friends, with little context about why each one wants to run.

Once approved, it's the pnpm I like, fast and strict. Maybe, until the prompts explain themselves.
