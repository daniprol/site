The new build-script allowlist is the right default. Nothing runs on install unless I said it could, which is what I always wanted from a package manager and never bothered to configure.

The cost shows up on old projects. The first install is a wall of approvals, one per dependency that wants to compile something, and I approved most of them without reading. That is the opposite of what the feature is for.

I am keeping it on the new projects and leaving the old ones alone for now. I will come back to this one once I have migrated something bigger.
