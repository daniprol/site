Sample snack. I swapped Prettier for oxfmt in the pre-commit hook mostly out of curiosity. The hook used to be something I waited for. Now I cannot tell it ran.

The output matches Prettier closely enough that adopting it changed a handful of lines in the whole repository. It also sorts imports and Tailwind classes, which let me delete two plugins.

Editor support is thinner than Prettier's, so format-on-save took a little setup. After that it disappeared, which is what I want from a formatter. Merged.
