Sample snack. Same output as Prettier on my TypeScript and CSS, which matters more than the speed: switching produced a diff of zero lines across the repo.

The speed is the fun part. Formatting the whole site went from a pause long enough to notice to something that finishes before the key comes back up.

Astro support is new, so I checked every template by hand the first time. Nothing broke, and the pre-commit hook is fast again. It runs as `oxfmt --check` in CI and nobody has complained since.
