Sample snack. The first two minutes are great. Good type, a calm empty state, and a demo note that explains the idea better than the landing page does.

Then I asked it to summarise one meeting note, and it asked for my calendar, my contacts and my mail so it could "add context". I said no, and the summary I got was the first sentence of each paragraph.

There is no export either, so anything I wrote there would stay there. Uninstalled the same afternoon.
