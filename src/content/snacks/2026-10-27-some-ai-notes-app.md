Sample snack. The first two minutes are great. Good type, a calm empty state, and a demo note that explains the idea better than the landing page does.

I gave it a week of meeting notes and asked for summaries. They are not clever, but they are short, they keep the decisions, and they saved me from rereading two long ones before a call.

It goes in the toolbox for now. I have not looked at where the notes live or how to get them out, which is the first thing I should have checked.
