Sample snack. Opening a large repo is instant, and typing has no lag at all. After a day in it my usual editor felt like it was wading through something.

The gaps are small but daily: one debugger workflow I rely on, and a couple of extensions that have no equivalent yet.

Maybe. I'm keeping it for reading code and quick edits, and checking back in a few months.
